What Is a 230V Calculator

A 230V Calculator is an electrical calculator for 230 volt circuits. The calculator solves amps, watts, kilowatts, kVA, and power demand from known values such as voltage, current, and PF. 230V Amps Calculation Formula

The 230V amps calculation formula is amps = watts / (230 x PF) for single phase AC. For DC at 230V, use amps = watts / 230. For AC - Three phase with line voltage, use amps = watts / (1.732 x 230 x PF).

To convert volts to amps at 230V, divide watts by 230 and power factor. Voltage alone cannot become amps. The conversion needs Power in Watts, apparent power in kVA, or resistance in ohms.

Is 230V Enough Information to Calculate Watts?

No. 230V is voltage only. Calculate watts with Current in Amps and Power Factor for AC loads.

How Many Watts Is 230V?

230V equals 230 W at 1 A and PF 1. 230V equals 2,300 W at 10 A and PF 1.

Can a 12V to 230V Calculator Size an Inverter?

Yes. Use load watts, inverter efficiency, battery voltage, and runtime to estimate DC current draw and battery size for a 12V to 230V inverter system.

Do 230V Breaker Sizes Depend on Watts Only?

No. Breaker size depends on current, load duration, conductor ampacity, installation method, temperature, voltage drop, and local electrical code.

230V Calculator FAQ

Fast answers before you use the calculator or compare formulas.

W How do I convert amps to watts at 230V?

Multiply amps by 230: Watts = Amps × 230 × PF. For a kettle drawing 13 amps at 230V with PF 1.0: 13 × 230 = 2,990 watts. The UK standard ring main circuit uses a 32A breaker, handling up to 7,360 watts.

13A What is the maximum watts on a UK 13A socket?

A UK BS 1363 socket with a 13A fused plug can deliver 2,990 watts maximum (13 × 230). In practice, this limits individual appliances to about 3 kW. For higher loads like electric showers (7–10.5 kW), a dedicated circuit with higher-rated MCB is required.

EU Is European voltage 220V or 230V?

The EU harmonized to a nominal 230V ±10% in 2003 (EN 50160). Previously, most of continental Europe used 220V and the UK used 240V. The wide tolerance means actual voltage can range from 207V to 253V. Both old 220V and 240V equipment works safely within this range.

PF Do UK appliances use power factor?

Resistive UK appliances (kettles, toasters, immersion heaters) have PF ≈ 1.00. Motor-driven appliances (washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners) have PF ranging from 0.75 to 0.95. For load planning on a consumer unit, accounting for PF gives more accurate watt calculations.

SHW How many amps does a UK electric shower use?

A 9.5 kW electric shower at 230V draws 9,500 ÷ 230 = 41.3 amps. This requires a dedicated 45A MCB and 10mm² cable. Higher-powered showers (10.5 kW) draw about 45.7 amps. Always check the installation complies with BS 7671 wiring regulations.

MCB What MCB size do I need for my watt load at 230V?

Divide your total watts by 230 to get amps, then select the next standard MCB size above. For example, 4,600W ÷ 230 = 20A, so a 20A Type B MCB would suit a resistive load. For motor loads, use a Type C MCB to handle startup inrush. Always have an electrician verify the circuit design.
