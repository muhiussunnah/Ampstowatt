Amp Hours to Watt Hours Calculator: Battery Capacity Conversion

An amp hours to watt hours calculator converts battery capacity from ampere-hours (Ah) to watt-hours (Wh) by multiplying by the nominal voltage. This essential calculation determines how much energy a battery can store and deliver.

There are 4 key applications for Ah to Wh conversion: solar battery system sizing, electric vehicle range calculation, portable power bank capacity assessment, and energy storage system planning. The 3 most common mistakes are forgetting voltage variations, ignoring efficiency losses, and miscalculating runtime without considering discharge rates.

Battery energy diagram

Ah to Wh Battery Capacity Flow

The calculator treats amp-hours as stored charge and voltage as system pressure. Multiplying both values gives watt-hours, the energy value used for runtime, inverter sizing, and solar storage planning.

Amp Hours to Watt Hours Formulas

Basic Conversion

Wh = Ah × V

Where:
Wh = Watt-hours
Ah = Amp-hours
V = Voltage

With Efficiency

Wh = Ah × V × (η ÷ 100)

Where:
η = Efficiency percentage

Energy Over Time

E = P × t

Where:
E = Energy (Wh)
P = Power (W)
t = Time (hours)

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert amp hours to watt hours?

Watt-hours = Amp-hours × Voltage. For example, a 100Ah 12V battery stores 100 × 12 = 1200Wh.

What is the difference between Ah and Wh?

Amp-hours measure electrical charge capacity, watt-hours measure energy capacity. Voltage bridges the two units.

Why is efficiency important in battery calculations?

Batteries have internal losses. A battery rated at 100Ah might only deliver 85-95Ah usable capacity due to Peukert effect and temperature.
