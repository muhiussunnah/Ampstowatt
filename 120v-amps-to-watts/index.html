FAQ

120V Calculator FAQ

Fast answers before you use the calculator or compare formulas.

15A How many watts is 15 amps at 120V?

15 amps at 120V equals 1,800 watts (15 × 120 = 1,800). This is the maximum capacity of a standard 15A household circuit. For continuous loads, the NEC 80% rule limits you to 1,440 watts on this circuit.

20A How many watts is 20 amps at 120V?

20 amps at 120V equals 2,400 watts. Kitchen and bathroom outlets are typically on 20A circuits. The continuous load limit is 1,920 watts (80% of 2,400).

MAX What is the maximum wattage for a 120V outlet?

A standard 120V, 15A outlet can handle 1,800 watts maximum (15 × 120). A 120V, 20A outlet handles 2,400 watts. However, for devices running more than 3 hours continuously, stay below the 80% threshold: 1,440W on a 15A circuit, 1,920W on a 20A circuit.

240 Why do some appliances need 240V instead of 120V?

High-power appliances like dryers (5,500W), EV chargers (7,680W), and central AC (3,600W) need more watts than a 120V circuit can safely deliver. At 120V, a 5,500W dryer would draw 46 amps — far exceeding standard circuit limits. At 240V, it draws only 23 amps, which fits a 30A circuit safely.

PF Do I need power factor for 120V household calculations?

For most household loads (lights, heaters, toasters, hair dryers), power factor is 1.00 or very close, so the simple formula Watts = Amps × 120 is sufficient. Use power factor only for motor-driven appliances like refrigerators (PF 0.80–0.90), window AC units (PF 0.85), and large workshop tools.

OUT How many 120V outlets can I put on one circuit?

The NEC does not set a fixed number of outlets per circuit, but the total load must stay within the breaker rating. A 15A/120V circuit handles 1,800W max. Add up the wattage of everything plugged in. In practice, 8–10 general-purpose outlets on a 15A circuit is common when not all are loaded simultaneously.
