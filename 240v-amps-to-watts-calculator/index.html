The 240V amps to watts formula is watts = amps x 240 for DC and resistive AC loads. For AC appliances with motors, transformers, or compressors, use watts = amps x 240 x Power Factor. This Amps to Watts Formula is the base of the Amps to Watts calculation.

How to Calculate Watts From Amps at 240V

To calculate watts from amps at 240V, multiply amps by 240 and multiply by Power Factor for AC loads. The direct formula is P = V x I, where Voltage (V) is 240 and Current (Amps) is the measured or rated current.

Voltage Current Watt Relationship

The voltage current watt relationship is Power (Watts) = Voltage (V) x Current (Amps). Ohm's Law connects volts, amps, ohms, and watts when resistance is known. A Multimeter measures Voltage. A Clamp Meter measures Current.

240V Current Calculation Formula

The 240V current calculation formula is amps = watts / 240 for resistive loads. The AC formula is amps = watts / (240 x PF). Three-Phase Power uses amps = watts / (1.732 x volts x PF).

What Makes a 240V Load Different From 120V?

A 240V load delivers twice the watts of a 120V load at the same amperage. For example, 10 A at 120V equals 1,200 W, while 10 A at 240V equals 2,400 W at PF 1.
