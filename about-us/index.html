About Ampstowatt.com

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Ampstowatt.com is an independent educational website built to help users understand, calculate, and verify common electrical power conversions. Our primary goal is simple: make amps-to-watts calculations, AC/DC power formulas, battery energy estimates, volt-amp conversions, voltage drop checks, power factor concepts, and wire sizing references easier to access in one clear place.

Our calculators and written guides are designed for homeowners, students, electricians, solar and battery learners, technicians, engineers, and anyone who needs fast reference math for electrical planning. We focus on plain-language explanations, transparent formulas, and practical examples so users can understand the result instead of only seeing a number.

Our Mission

Electrical calculations can affect safety, budgeting, equipment selection, and energy planning. Our mission is to reduce confusion by presenting common formulas in a structured, accessible, and mobile-friendly format. Ampstowatt.com does not replace a licensed electrician or qualified engineer, but it helps users prepare better questions, check basic values, and understand the relationship between current, voltage, power, apparent power, energy, and power factor.

What We Offer

  • Amps to watts calculator: Convert current to real power for DC, single-phase AC, and three-phase AC circuits.
  • Amp-hours to watt-hours guidance: Estimate stored or consumed energy for batteries, portable power stations, solar systems, and runtime planning.
  • Volt-amps and power factor tools: Understand the difference between real power in watts, apparent power in VA, and reactive power concepts.
  • Watt-to-amps conversion: Reverse the formula to estimate current draw from known watts and voltage.
  • Voltage drop and wire gauge references: Learn how current, conductor material, distance, and installation conditions influence electrical performance.
  • Educational resources: Read formulas, worked examples, reference tables, safety notes, and definitions written for non-specialists and professionals alike.

Accuracy and Calculation Methodology

We use standard electrical relationships such as Watts = Amps × Volts for DC circuits, Watts = Amps × Volts × Power Factor for single-phase AC circuits, and Watts = √3 × Amps × Volts × Power Factor for three-phase line-to-line AC calculations. Where assumptions are necessary, we clearly state them in the tool or supporting content.

Electrical systems vary by country, code, equipment rating, conductor type, temperature, duty cycle, and installation method. For safety-critical work, permit decisions, final conductor sizing, load center work, industrial installations, or code compliance, always verify calculations with current local regulations and a licensed professional.
