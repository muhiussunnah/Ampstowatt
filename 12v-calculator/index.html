How Does a 12V Calculator Work?

A 12V Calculator works by applying Ohm's law and power formulas to a fixed 12 volt source voltage. Power uses W = V x A, current uses A = W / V, and battery energy uses Wh = Ah x V. Wire sizing adds resistivity, one-way length, maximum current, conductor material, and allowable voltage drop.

12V Power Formula

Fx

The 12V power formula is Watts = Amps x 12 Volts. The same formula works for direct current (DC) loads and resistive 12 volt devices. Alternating current (AC) loads use power factor when the 12V value comes from an inverter or transformer.

W = A x 12A = W / 12Wh = Ah x 12

Ohm's law connects resistance to 12V calculations: V = I x R. A 12V load drawing 5 amps has an equivalent resistance of 2.4 ohms, using R = 12 / 5.

The 12V wattage formula is wattage = 12 volts x current in amperes. Use this formula for power consumption in DC circuits, 12V appliances, LED lighting, and battery-powered equipment.

12V Calculator FAQ
How many watts is 5 amps at 12 volts?

5 amps at 12 volts is 60 watts. Use watts = amps x volts, so 5 x 12 = 60.

How many amps is 100 watts at 12 volts?

100 watts at 12 volts is 8.33 amps. Use amps = watts / volts, so 100 / 12 = 8.33.

How long will a 12V 100 Ah battery run a 60 W load?

A 12V 100 Ah battery runs a 60 W load for 20 hours in a theoretical full-capacity calculation. Use 16 hours when 80% of 1200 Wh is usable.

Can I use 16 AWG wire for 12 volts?

Yes, 16 AWG wire can work for small 12V loads over short runs. At 5 meters, 3% voltage drop, and 50 C, a reference calculation limits 16 AWG near 2.5 amperes.

What is the 12V battery watt hours formula?

The 12V battery watt hours formula is Wh = Ah x 12 V. A 50 Ah battery stores 600 Wh, and a 100 Ah battery stores 1200 Wh before usable-capacity limits.

Is this the same as a solar-powered handheld calculator?

No, this 12V Calculator is an electrical planning tool for 12 volt systems. A Texas Instruments TI-30X, Casio fx-260, or Sharp EL-501X is a handheld electronic calculator with a solar cell, battery compartment, LCD display, keypad, and circuit board.
