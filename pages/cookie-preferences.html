This page explains the cookie choices available to AmpereToWatt.com users. Some controls may be provided through a consent banner or privacy message depending on your location and which services are active on the website.

Cookie Categories

These support basic website operation, security, consent storage, and page delivery. They are generally always active because the site may not work properly without them.

Preference Cookies

These remember choices such as language, display options, or interface preferences if such features are enabled.

Choice: You may disable these in your browser or consent settings where available.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand which pages are useful, how visitors find the site, and how we can improve performance and content quality.

Choice: You may reject analytics cookies through a consent tool where available or block them in your browser.

Advertising Cookies

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are active, advertising cookies may support ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and personalized ads where permitted.

How to Change Your Choices

  1. Use the cookie banner or consent management panel if it appears for your region.
  2. Clear cookies for AmpereToWatt.com in your browser to reset saved choices.
  3. Use browser settings to block third-party cookies or delete stored cookies.
  4. Review Google ad personalization settings if Google advertising services are active.

Important Notes

  • Consent choices may be stored in a cookie or local storage so the website can remember your selection.
  • Clearing browser data may remove your saved choices and cause consent prompts to reappear.
  • Blocking all cookies may reduce functionality or prevent preference storage.
  • Some third-party controls are managed by the third party, not directly by AmpereToWatt.com.
