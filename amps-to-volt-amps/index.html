AMPS TO VOLT-AMPS

Amps to Volt-Amps Calculator: Apparent Power Conversion

An amps to volt-amps calculator converts electrical current in amperes to apparent power in volt-amps (VA) by applying voltage. This calculation is essential for sizing electrical equipment and understanding power system capacity.

There are 3 key applications for VA calculations: transformer sizing, electrical service planning, and equipment capacity verification. The 2 most common misconceptions are confusing VA with watts, and forgetting phase configuration affects the calculation.

Apparent power diagram

Current and Voltage to VA Flow

The calculator multiplies current by voltage to find apparent power. Apparent power helps size transformers, UPS systems, generators, and service panels before power factor is applied to real watts.
