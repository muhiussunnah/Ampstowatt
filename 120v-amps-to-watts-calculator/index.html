120V Amps to Watts FAQ
How many watts is 10 amps at 120V?

10 amps at 120V is 1200 watts at power factor 1. Use 10 x 120 = 1200 W.

How many watts is 15 amps at 120V?

15 amps at 120V is 1800 watts at power factor 1. This is a common 15-amp circuit size table value.

How many watts is 20 amps at 120V?

20 amps at 120V is 2400 watts at power factor 1. Continuous load rules may use a lower planning value.

Does power factor matter at 120V?

Yes, power factor matters for AC motors, compressors, LED drivers, and electronic supplies. Resistive loads often calculate close to PF 1.
