A DC Calculator for Electrical Engineering solves direct current equations used in batteries, solar cells, electronics, rectifier circuits, and power supply distribution systems. DC power flows in one direction. AC power, or alternating current, changes direction and is used in homes, businesses, and long-distance power transmission.

DC Voltage Drop Calculator

A DC voltage drop calculator finds lost voltage across a wire run. Use voltage drop = 2 x current x resistance x length for two-wire DC circuits. Voltage drop matters in solar panels, LED strips, battery banks, and low-voltage controls.

DC Wire Gauge Calculator

A DC wire gauge calculator chooses wire size from amps, length, voltage, and allowable voltage drop. 12V and 24V systems need careful wire sizing since low voltage creates higher current for the same watts.

DC Amp Calculator

A DC amp calculator finds current with Idc = Pdc / Vdc. A 120 W DC load at 12 V draws 10 A. A 120 W load at 24 V draws 5 A. Units can be A, mA, or kA depending on system size.

DC Power Calculation Online

DC power calculation online uses Pdc = Idc x Vdc. Example Problem #1 uses 51 A and 6 V. The result is 51 x 6 = 306 watts. Convert 306 W to 0.306 kW or 0.000306 MW.

DC Circuit Calculator

A DC circuit calculator applies Ohm's law to series and parallel circuits. Use V = I x R, I = V / R, and P = V x I for resistors, diodes, transistors, power consumption, and electronic calculator circuit board design.

DC Fuse Calculator

A DC fuse calculator sizes fuse protection from load current and circuit type. Many DC loads use a fuse above normal current and below wire ampacity. Startup surge events, constant current loads, and power supply inrush current affect fuse choice.

DC Load Calculator

A DC load calculator totals watts, amps, and runtime for connected devices. Add each load, multiply by hours, and convert to watt-hours. This helps estimate battery size, power supply rating, and distribution systems capacity.

Specialized DC calculators solve specific direct current problems for motors, batteries, cables, and DC to AC conversion. These tools support electrical and electronic systems during power consumption checks, component selection, and power supply design.

DC Motor Calculator

A DC motor calculator estimates motor power, current, torque, and efficiency. BLDC Motor Power Calculator and DC Motor Torque Calculator workflows use voltage, current, speed, torque, and losses. Startup surge events and temperature derating curves can change the current draw.

DC Battery Calculator

A DC battery calculator estimates runtime from battery capacity and load power. Battery watt-hours = volts x amp-hours. Runtime = battery watt-hours / load watts. Use this for batteries, solar cells, electronics, off-grid loads, and backup power systems.

DC Cable Size Calculator 12V

A DC cable size calculator 12V selects cable from amps, length, and voltage drop limit. 12V loads draw high current, so cable size, fuse rating, voltage drop, and connection resistance need careful checks before installation.

DC to AC Converter Calculator kW

A DC to AC converter calculator kW estimates inverter input current from AC output power and efficiency. A 1 kW AC load on a 12 V battery with 90 percent inverter efficiency draws about 92.6 A from the DC side. A rectifier performs the reverse direction by converting AC to DC with diodes, transistors, or other components.

What is a DC Calculator?

A DC Calculator finds direct current power, current, voltage, or resistance by using DC formulas such as Pdc = Idc x Vdc and Ohm's law.

What is the DC power formula?

The DC power formula is Pdc = Idc x Vdc, where Pdc is power in watts, Idc is current in amps, and Vdc is voltage in volts.

Does DC power use power factor?

No. Pure DC power calculations use watts = amps x volts and do not use AC power factor.

What does DC Calculator mean in D&D?

In D&D, DC means Difficulty Class. A DC calculator for DND or 5E helps set target numbers for ability checks, saving throws, and spell save DC.
